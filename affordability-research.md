# Affordability Hub Research Notes

## Key Statistics
- 68.5 million adults in the US don't have dental insurance (27% of adults)
- 31 million older Americans (65+) lack dental coverage
- Percentage without dental insurance (27%) is 3x higher than those without health insurance (9.3%)
- 2026 is being called "the breaking point" for affordable dental care in the US

## Common Dental Costs Without Insurance (2025-2026)
- Routine dental exam: $50–$350 (avg $203)
- Routine cleaning: $75–$200 (avg $130–$160)
- X-rays: $25–$250
- Cavity filling: $100–$1,050
- Tooth extraction: $50–$500 (simple)
- Crown: $800–$2,500
- Root canal: $700–$1,500
- Dental implant: $3,000–$6,000
- Braces: $2,500–$7,000
- Porcelain veneers: $1,600–$2,000
